Why honors

Built like a good margin note — close to the text, easy to return to.

Honors at Snow isn't an add-on major. It's a running conversation across your two years here: interdisciplinary seminars, a faculty mentor from day one, and a thesis that's actually yours.

Every honors student keeps the same advisor for both years, sits in discussion-first classrooms capped at 18, and leaves with departmental distinction on the diploma.

  1. 01

    Seminars, not lectures

    Every honors course runs as discussion. You'll talk more than you'll be talked at.

  2. 02

    One mentor, two years

    A faculty advisor who knows your work from your first essay to your capstone.

  3. 03

    Distinction on record

    Honors designation and thesis appear on your official Snow College transcript.
